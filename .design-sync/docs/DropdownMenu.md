Menu of actions anchored to a trigger. Compose `<DropdownMenu>` with `<DropdownMenuTrigger asChild>` and `<DropdownMenuContent>` holding `<DropdownMenuItem>`, `<DropdownMenuCheckboxItem>`, `<DropdownMenuRadioItem>`, plus `<DropdownMenuLabel>` and `<DropdownMenuSeparator>` for structure.
